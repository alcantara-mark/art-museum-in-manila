GSIS Museo ng Sining

GSIS Museo ng Sining is a cultural gem located within the GSIS Complex in Pasay City. It houses an extensive collection of classic and contemporary Filipino art, including impressive monumental paintings and intricate sculptures.

Perfect for art lovers, museum dates, quiet photography walks, or finding inspiration surrounded by rich Filipino heritage and elegant gallery space.
