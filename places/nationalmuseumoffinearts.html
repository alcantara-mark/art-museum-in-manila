National Museum of Fine Arts

The National Museum of Fine Arts is an iconic neoclassical landmark in Ermita, Manila. It houses legendary masterpieces including Juan Luna's Spoliarium, along with grand hallways, high ceilings, and rich historical galleries.

Perfect for museum dates, classic portrait photography, historical walks, or finding aesthetic outfit inspiration surrounded by timeless Filipino art.
