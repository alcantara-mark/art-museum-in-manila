About Mark

I'm Mark—a Manila-based fashion influencer passionate about scouting stylish art spots and curating outfits that match every gallery's aesthetic.

IG Worthy Spots is your personal guide to discovering the most aesthetic art museums and cultural spaces around Metro Manila. I curate locations featuring striking architecture, creative gallery layouts, historic halls, and hidden spaces that make the perfect backdrop for your photos.

My goal is to help you discover new gallery spaces, plan your next photo shoot, and find outfit inspiration that matches the unique aesthetic of Manila's best art spots.
